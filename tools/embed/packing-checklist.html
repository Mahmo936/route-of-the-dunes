🏜️ Sahara Packing Checklist

Clothing layers
  • ☐ Breathable long sleeves (sun protection)
  • ☐ Fleece or warm mid-layer for cold nights
  • ☐ Windproof jacket (winter mornings near 0°C)
  • ☐ Closed shoes + socks you don't mind sanding up
Sun & dust essentials
  • ☐ High-SPF sunscreen & lip balm
  • ☐ Sunglasses (category 3-4)
  • ☐ Scarf or buff for wind and sand
  • ☐ Refillable water bottle (min 1.5L)
Smart extras
  • ☐ Power bank (no sockets at camp)
  • ☐ Headlamp for the camp night
  • ☐ Small backpack for the camel trek
  • ☐ Wet wipes — showers are limited
