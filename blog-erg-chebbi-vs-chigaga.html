Erg Chebbi or Erg Chigaga: Which Desert Should You Choose

Morocco has two great dune seas, not one. Here's honestly how they compare, and why our routes are built around Erg Chebbi.

6 min read · Comparison

"The Sahara" in Morocco usually means one of two places: Erg Chebbi, near Merzouga, or Erg Chigaga, near M'Hamid further south. Both are genuine Saharan dune seas with the same golden sand and the same star-filled sky — the real differences are in how far you have to go to reach them, and what that distance buys you.

Side by side

Why our routes go to Erg Chebbi

Both of our routes — the 3-day loop and the 5-day Fes crossing — end at Erg Chebbi, and that's a deliberate choice, not a default. For a trip in the 3-5 day range that most travelers actually have available, Erg Chebbi is the dune sea that fits: it's reachable on a road that lets a normal vehicle make good time, the drive itself passes through some of the country's best scenery (Aït Ben Haddou, Todra Gorge, the Dadès Valley), and once you arrive, the dunes rise directly and dramatically from the edge of Merzouga — no further trek required to get the classic view.

Erg Chigaga's remoteness is genuinely part of its appeal, but it's also exactly what makes it a poor fit for a shorter trip — the extra day or two it takes to reach and return from M'Hamid would eat directly into the time you'd otherwise spend actually at the dunes, or elsewhere in Morocco.

Both are "real" Sahara. Erg Chebbi being more visited doesn't make it less authentic — it's still a genuine dune sea on the edge of the Sahara, just one that a well-built road happens to reach.

When Erg Chigaga makes more sense

If you have six or more days specifically for a desert-focused trip, and remoteness matters more to you than efficiency, Chigaga is worth considering with an operator who specializes in that route. It genuinely sees fewer visitors, and the extra effort to reach it is part of what travelers who choose it are looking for. It's simply a different trip than the 3-5 day itineraries we run — not a better or worse one.

The honest bottom line

If you're weighing this while planning a first Morocco trip with a handful of days to spare, Erg Chebbi via one of our routes gets you the full Sahara experience — the dunes, the camel trek, the night under the stars — without needing to sacrifice the rest of the country to reach it. Chigaga is for a different kind of trip, not a better version of the same one.

Quick answers

A few follow-up questions travelers ask about this.

Can I visit both Erg Chebbi and Erg Chigaga on the same trip?

Not comfortably on a standard 3-5 day trip — combining both would mean over a week just for the desert portion, since they sit roughly a day's drive apart in opposite directions from the main route.

Is Erg Chebbi worth it if I've seen dunes elsewhere?

Yes — the height and accessibility of Erg Chebbi's dunes, combined with the drive through Aït Ben Haddou and the Dadès Valley to reach them, make the journey itself part of the value, not just the sand.

How much taller are Erg Chebbi's dunes than Erg Chigaga's?

Erg Chebbi's tallest dunes reach around 150 meters, generally taller than most of Erg Chigaga's, though Chigaga's dune field spreads across a wider area.
