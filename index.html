Sample itineraries

What each route actually looks like.

Two routes, two shapes — pick a tab to see the day-by-day. Adjust stops, timings and meals to match your own trip.

01Day

Marrakesh → Aït Ben Haddou → Ouarzazate → Dadès Valley

Hotel pickup in Marrakesh, then cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n'Tichka pass with a stop for mint tea along the way, visit the UNESCO kasbah of Aït Ben Haddou, pass the film studios of Ouarzazate, and arrive in Dadès Valley by evening.

~7h on the road
Lunch, dinner
02Day

Dadès → Todra Gorge → Erg Chebbi

Morning walk into Todra Gorge between its towering canyon walls, with a stop at the nearby Tinghir oasis, then drive on to Merzouga for a sunset camel trek into the dunes, dinner around the fire with live Berber music, and a night at the desert camp.

~5h on the road
Breakfast, dinner
03Day

Sunrise → Sandboarding → Merzouga → Marrakesh

Early wake-up for sunrise over the dunes with a quick sandboarding session right there before breakfast, then hit the road promptly for the long drive back to Marrakesh, with a stop in Ouarzazate along the way.

~7h on the road
Breakfast
01Day

Fes → Ifrane → Azrou Cedar Forest → Ziz Valley

Cross the Middle Atlas through Ifrane (the "Switzerland of Morocco") and stop in the Azrou cedar forest to see the Barbary macaques, then continue south to overnight in the Ziz Valley.

~7h on the road
Lunch, dinner
02Day

Ziz Valley → Merzouga → Erg Chebbi

Follow the Ziz palm groves down into the desert, arrive in Merzouga by early afternoon, then head out for a camel trek to the heart of the dunes at sunset, dinner around the fire with Berber music and dance, and a night at the desert camp.

~4h on the road
Breakfast, dinner
03Day

Sunrise at the dunes → Merzouga → free time

Watch the sunrise from camp, ride back out of the dunes, and spend the rest of the day around Merzouga — try on traditional Berber dress for photos, get a henna tattoo, or add an optional evening like a Khamlia Gnawa music night or a fossil workshop in Erfoud — before overnighting locally.

Free time
Breakfast, dinner
04Day

Merzouga → Todra Gorge → Dadès Valley

Drive north to walk into Todra Gorge between its canyon walls, then continue to the Dadès Valley (the "Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs") for the night.

~6h on the road
Breakfast, dinner
05Day

Dadès Valley → Ouarzazate → Marrakesh

Cross back over the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, with a stop in Ouarzazate, arriving in Marrakesh by evening — with time for a short guided walk through the medina if you land early enough.

~7h on the road
Breakfast

Before you book

Frequently asked questions.

The four questions almost every traveler asks before requesting a quote.

What's actually included in the price?

Transport, driver-guide, accommodation at each stop, and the meals listed on each route (usually breakfast and dinner). Lunch and drinks are typically not included, so you can choose where to eat along the way. We'll confirm the exact list for your specific quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payment via PayPal, Wise (bank transfer), and card payment. Once your trip details are confirmed, we'll send you a secure payment link or transfer details for whichever method suits you best.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and deposit terms are confirmed with your individual quote — see our Terms of Service for the general terms, or ask us directly when you request a quote.

How physically demanding is the trip?

Mostly vehicle travel with short walks — the camel trek is gentle, and Todra Gorge involves an easy walk on a flat canyon floor. Suitable for most fitness levels; let your guide know in advance if anyone in your group has mobility needs.

When's the best time of year to go?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the most comfortable, with warm days and cool nights. Summer is hot but manageable with an air-conditioned vehicle; winter nights at the dunes can be cold, so camps provide extra blankets and heaters.
