Morocco Tourism Statistics (2025–2026): Sources & Sahara Context

A citable reference of verified Morocco tourism figures — with publisher links, definitions, and clear limits for desert/Drâa-Tafilalet context. Updated 16 November 2026.

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · Each figure includes publisher, period, geography, and source link

This page collects official, source-linked tourism statistics for Morocco and explains how to read them when planning or writing about the Sahara. It is not a generic roundup: every figure below states what it measures, where, when, and who published it.

Scope and limits: National figures apply to Morocco as published. There is no official Merzouga or Erg Chebbi visitor series in the sources compiled here — desert-related context must use regional or city proxies (e.g. Drâa-Tafilalet, Ouarzazate, Zagora, Errachidia) and be labeled as such. Unofficial camp capacity or price claims are not included as official statistics.

3) Regional / Sahara context (proxies only)

Travelers often ask for “Merzouga numbers.” The correct, citable approach is to use regional or city proxies and label them clearly.

  • No official Merzouga or Erg Chebbi visitor totals are available in the compiled sources. Do not attribute Morocco-wide or regional statistics to Merzouga or Erg Chebbi.
  • Drâa-Tafilalet (background): The official regional monograph reports a 2014 (RGPH) population of 1,635,008 (34.30% urban) for the Drâa-Tafilalet region — Monographie de la région de Drâa-Tafilalet (PDF). This is background context only and must not be presented as current.
  • City-level hotel performance (Ouarzazate, Zagora, Errachidia): The Observatoire’s Rapports des performances hôtelières page showed no publicly listed publications at collection time (15 Nov 2026); it should be re-checked after login for city-level occupancy/overnight-stay data.
How to cite desert context: Prefer phrasing such as “in the Drâa-Tafilalet region” or “a proxy for the Ouarzazate–Zagora–Errachidia area.” Keep geography labels explicit in every claim.

4) Methodology and data-quality rules

  • Source per figure: every number on this page includes value, indicator, geography, period, and publisher link.
  • No mixed geographies: national, regional, and city figures are not interchangeable.
  • Definitions differ: arrivals, receipts, travel receipts, and tourism consumption are different measures; compare like with like.
  • No unofficial totals as “official”: desert camp capacity and prices are not published as official statistics and are excluded here.
  • Pending ≠ confirmed: items awaiting account download or access verification are listed but not quoted as figures.

Quick answers

Common questions about these statistics and their limits.

Are there official visitor numbers for Merzouga or Erg Chebbi?

No. The compiled official sources do not provide a Merzouga or Erg Chebbi visitor series. Use regional/city proxies (Drâa-Tafilalet, Ouarzazate, Zagora, Errachidia) and label them clearly.

Why do receipt totals differ (138 vs 138.6 billion MAD)?

They come from different publishers/series (Ministry foreign-currency tourism receipts vs Bank Al-Maghrib travel receipts). This page keeps them separate rather than merging them.

Will this page add monthly desert-season figures?

Yes, once Observatoire monthly tables are downloaded (free account) and verified. Any additions will keep the same per-figure sourcing and geography labels.
